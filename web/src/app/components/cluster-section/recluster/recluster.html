Merges clusters from two separately clustered populations and computes pairwise Levenshtein Edit Distance between cluster seeds. Use Multi-round mode to track cluster family convergence across 3 selection rounds.

Avg RPU — mean RPU across all sequences assigned to the super-cluster in that population. Reflects the overall abundance of the cluster family, but is pulled down by low-read peripheral variants. Seed RPU — RPU of the original cluster seed (rank 1 sequence in the original cluster file, i.e. the most abundant sequence in that cluster). More robust than Avg RPU as it represents the dominant variant, unaffected by rare sequences at the cluster periphery. When to use each: Use Seed RPU to compare the dominant aptamer between rounds. Use Avg RPU to capture the overall enrichment of the entire cluster family.
